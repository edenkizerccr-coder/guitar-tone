# Guitar Tone Windows beta

I wanted a guitar amp simulator with a cleaner keyboard workflow for JAWS, so I built Guitar Tone. I’ve tried Guitar Tone with JAWS and would love more testers. Feedback from any Windows user is welcome.

- [Download Guitar Tone for Windows x64](https://github.com/edenkizerccr-coder/guitar-tone/releases/download/v0.10.0-beta.1/Guitar-Tone-Windows-x64-v0.10.0-beta.1.zip)
- [Download the Guitar Tone Word manual](https://github.com/edenkizerccr-coder/guitar-tone/releases/download/v0.10.0-beta.1/Guitar-Tone-User-Manual.docx)

Extract the ZIP and open **Guitar Tone.exe** from the extracted folder. Keep the **Amp Library** folder beside the program. Press **F6** for the preset list, use the arrows to browse, press **F3** to hear a preset without plugging in, and press **Enter** to load it. **Ctrl+F1** opens the Word manual.

The beta includes 973 presets, a 32-slot pedalboard with pitch shift and 28 NAM pedal and preamp captures, two cabinets with stereo spread, recorded-space reverb, a looper, and WAV recording. Press **Ctrl+Shift+P** for Pedalboard, **Ctrl+Shift+B** for Cabinet Mixer, or **Ctrl+Shift+R** for Convolution Reverb.

I’d especially like to know whether your screen reader speaks the preset name as you move through the list, and how pitch shift sounds on full chords. If you hit a problem, tell me what you pressed and what you heard. Include your Windows and screen reader versions if you know them. [Share beta feedback on GitHub](https://github.com/edenkizerccr-coder/guitar-tone/issues)
